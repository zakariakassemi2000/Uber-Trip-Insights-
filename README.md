# 🚗 Uber Trip Insights | Executive Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze Uber trip data, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and uncover actionable insights into trip demand, revenue, vehicle performance, and geographical patterns.

## 📊 Project Overview
The Uber Trip Insights Dashboard provides an executive-level overview of ride-hailing operations through interactive visualizations and performance indicators.

Built with a focus on data-driven decision-making, the dashboard enables users to explore trip activity over a selected period, compare vehicle categories, analyze payment methods, and identify high-demand pickup and drop-off locations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall trip volume and revenue performance.
- Analyze average fares, trip distances, and trip durations.
- Compare vehicle categories based on trips, revenue, and distance.
- Understand payment method preferences.
- Identify daily demand patterns and peak hours.
- Discover the most frequent pickup and drop-off locations.
- Support operational planning through data-driven insights.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators
The dashboard highlights the following KPIs:

- Total Trips: 104K

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
A consolidated view of business performance, including total trips, revenue, average fare, trip distance, trip duration, and revenue per mile.

### 2. Time Analysis
Exploration of daily trip activity and demand patterns to identify fluctuations and periods of increased demand.

### 3. Trip Details
Detailed analysis of trip characteristics, payment methods, vehicle categories, and operational metrics.

### 4. Vehicle Type Analysis
Comparison of UberX, UberXL, Uber Green, Uber Comfort, and Uber Black based on trip volume, revenue, average fare, and total distance.

### 5. Geographic & Location Analysis
Identification of the most frequent pickup and drop-off locations, along with popular pickup areas and preferred vehicle categories.

### 6. Interactive Filtering
Date and city filters allow users to explore the available data across different reporting periods and locations.

## 💡 Key Insights
Based on the displayed dashboard:

- Trip Volume: 104K total trips are recorded in the selected period.
- Revenue: Total trip revenue reaches approximately $1.6 million.
- Vehicle Preference: UberX leads the displayed vehicle categories with 38.7K trips.
- Trip Distribution: Day trips account for 72.8% of recorded trips.
- Peak Hours: The dashboard identifies 3–5 PM as the peak demand period.
- Geographic Patterns: Penn Station/Madison Sq West is the most frequent pickup location shown.
